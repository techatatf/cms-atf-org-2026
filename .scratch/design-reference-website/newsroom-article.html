Google.org backs the ATF AI Challenge to scale to four countries

The Challenge began in Ghana in 2023. With Google.org support, it will run in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, and South Africa.

Published[Date]
LocationAccra, Ghana
Reading time3 minutes
Contactpress@africantechnologyforum.org

The African Technology Forum's AI Challenge will scale to four countries — Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, and South Africa — with support from Google.org.

The Challenge trains university students in applied AI, then sets them real problems from partner organisations to build against. ATF ran the first Challenge in Ghana in 2023, with sponsorship from OpenAI.

From one country to four

In 2024, 450 students from 23 universities across Ghana took part and built 22 minimum viable products. In 2025 the Challenge expanded to Nigeria, and for the first time institutions brought their own problem statements.

  • Korle Bu Teaching Hospital — health.
  • 37 Military Hospital — health.
  • Esoko — agriculture.

Part of a longer story

ATF was founded at MIT in 1988 by African students and young professionals. It began as a journal, grew into a professional network and consulting practice, and ran university chapters in Ghana and Benin from 2012 to 2020. The Challenge continues the same work: finding technologists early and giving them a way in.

How to take part

Students can find dates and eligibility on atfchallenge.org. Organisations that want to set a problem statement or share mentors can contact the partnerships team.

Notes to editors

The African Technology Forum was founded in 1988 to advance the development of science and technology in Africa. It is registered in Ghana and operates in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, and South Africa through ATF Consulting, the ATF AI Challenge, and university chapters.
