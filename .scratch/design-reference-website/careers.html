Work with the forum.

ATF has been run by volunteers for most of its history. Volunteers mentor Challenge teams, support university chapters, write, and help run programmes in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, and South Africa. When paid roles open, we list them here.

Ways to Help Open Roles Volunteer

Ways to Help

Bring what you know.

Most volunteers give a few hours a month. Some take on a single project. All of them work directly with students and the ATF team.

Practitioners

Mentor a Challenge team

Guide a student team through the ATF AI Challenge, from problem statement to working MVP.

Experts

Judge or review

Assess Challenge submissions and give teams honest, specific feedback.

Academics & alumni

Support a chapter

Speak at, advise, or help set up a university chapter in your field.

Writers & editors

Write for ATF

ATF began as a journal in 1988. Help us report, edit, and publish the work of African technologists.

Organisers

Run events locally

Help deliver programmes and events in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, or South Africa.

Professionals

Lend a skill

Design, finance, legal, communications, or engineering support on a defined project.
