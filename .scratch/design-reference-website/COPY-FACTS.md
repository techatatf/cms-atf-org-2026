## Mission
- **Advancing the development of science and technology in Africa.** ("Advancing" replaces "promoting".)
- Supporting statement: "…by identifying and nurturing technologists across every discipline and career stage, building the community and knowledge infrastructure that connects them to each other and to the wider world, and creating the pathways that turn learning into lasting impact."
- Thread: ATF connects African technologists, students and professionals, to knowledge, to resources, and to each other.

## Identity
- Founded in 1988 at MIT by African students and young professionals. (Never 1987.)
- Registered in Ghana. Operates in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, and South Africa. Ran university chapters in Benin.
- Volunteer-run for most of its history.

## Eras
1. Journal era, 1988 to early 2000s: journal plus book drives to African university libraries. By 1992: about 23 volunteer staff, contributors in 15 countries, 11,000+ readers.
2. Consulting / professional network era, early 2000s to 2012: ATF Consulting. Still active, per the user.
3. University chapters era, 2012 to 2020: six chapters at peak (five in Ghana, one in Benin). Emerging-technology exposure such as nanotechnology and 3D printing.
4. ATF Challenge era, 2023 to today: 2023 first AI Challenge (OpenAI sponsorship) → 2024: 450 students, 23 universities (Ghana), 22 MVPs → 2025: expanded to Nigeria; Korle Bu Teaching Hospital, 37 Military Hospital, and Esoko as problem-statement partners → Google.org support to scale to four countries.

## Three pillars
Community · Programmes · Knowledge

## Positioning — not an AI organisation
- Strategic direction: "Ecosystem Architect". ATF designs and runs talent-to-impact pathways in whichever technology domain matters most at the time. AI is the current domain, not the identity.
- The mission is science-and-technology broad. History: journals → book drives → professional network → nanotechnology → 3D printing → AI.
- Domains to reference beyond AI: health, agriculture, industry and manufacturing, telecommunications, technology policy, core sciences, biotechnology.
- Rule for copy: only the AI Challenge page should be AI-led. Organisation-level pages (home, mission, consulting, chapters, publications) should say "science and technology".
