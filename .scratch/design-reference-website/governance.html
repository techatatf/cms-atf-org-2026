How ATF is governed.

ATF is registered in Ghana and governed by a Board of Directors. The board oversees ATF's strategy, finances, and accountability. Its members serve without pay.

Responsibilities

What the board does.

Mission and strategy

Sets ATF's direction and makes sure its programmes serve the mission.

Financial oversight

Approves budgets and reviews ATF's finances and use of funds.

Accountability

Reviews programme results and ATF's reporting to funders and partners.

Leadership

Appoints and supports ATF's leadership and new board members.

Structure

One organisation, four countries.

ATF operates as a single organisation, with country leads running programmes locally.
