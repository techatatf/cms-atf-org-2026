The ATF network, on your problem.

ATF Consulting puts the professional expertise of the ATF network to work for African institutions — in whichever field of science and technology the problem sits. It began in the early 2000s, when ATF's founding generation, by then senior in companies, universities, and institutions across Africa and the diaspora, started taking on projects together.

What We Do

Four kinds of work.

ATF has never been tied to one technology. Our work has moved from journals to nanotechnology to 3D printing to AI. Consulting follows the same logic: the field changes, the network stays.

01

Technology strategy & policy

Advice for ministries and institutions planning how science and technology will serve their goals — from national skills strategies to an institution's first technology plan.

ATF works in four countries, each developing national digital and technology skills strategies.

02

Skills & capability

Curriculum and training for your students or staff in the technical fields you need, built to be taught again once we hand it over.

ATF has introduced students to nanotechnology and 3D printing, and now designs and delivers AI training across four countries.

03

Sector expertise

Access to practitioners from the ATF network who work in your field — not a generalist pool.

The network spans founding-era professionals, former chapter members, and more than 120 mentors and experts in medicine, agriculture, finance, law, and engineering.

04

Problem definition & evaluation

Turning an institution's challenge into a problem technologists can work on — and measuring afterwards whether the solution worked.

Korle Bu Teaching Hospital, 37 Military Hospital, and Esoko have framed problem statements with ATF.

How We Work

From your problem to the right people.

Every engagement is staffed from the network and scoped to end with your team running the work.

01

Listen

We start with your problem, your constraints, and the people who will own the result.

02

Match

We find the practitioners in the ATF network who know your sector and have done this work before.

03

Deliver

They work alongside your staff, never as a parallel team.

04

Hand over

Your team leaves with the skills, documents, and ownership to carry on.

Where it comes from

1988.

A journal first. Then a network. Then a practice.

ATF began at MIT in 1988 as a journal connecting African scientists. By 1992 it had contributors in 15 countries and more than 11,000 readers. As those readers built careers across Africa and abroad, ATF Consulting grew out of the network to put their expertise to work at home.
