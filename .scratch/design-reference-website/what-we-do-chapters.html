What Chapters Do

A way in, on campus.

Chapters belong to ATF's Community pillar. A chapter is where a student first meets ATF — and often where they first meet someone already doing the work they want to do, in any field of science and technology.

01

Direction

Chapters get guidance from ATF on what to run and how, so students spend their time building rather than organising from scratch.

02

Access to experts

Introductions to researchers and practitioners from the ATF network, in the fields students are studying.

03

Emerging technology

Early exposure to new fields across science and technology. Past chapters explored nanotechnology and 3D printing. Today that includes AI, and chapters will follow whatever frontier comes next.

04

A lifelong community

Members join a community that spans ATF's founding generation, former chapter members, and today's programme participants, with a route into ATF programmes such as the AI Challenge.

History

Back to students, again.

ATF was founded by students at MIT in 1988. Chapters are how it keeps returning to them.

2012 to 2020

Six chapters at peak

Five chapters in Ghana and one in Benin. Each received funding, direction, and access to experts, and introduced students to fields like nanotechnology and 3D printing.

Today

Reactivating through universities

We are reopening chapters through memoranda of understanding with universities, so each chapter has an institutional home and outlasts any one student cohort.

How it works

1

Get in touch

Send us your university, your department, and the students or staff who want to lead.

2

Agree an MoU

ATF and the university sign a memorandum of understanding that sets out how the chapter will run.

3

Appoint student leads

The chapter is run by students, with a staff contact at the university.

4

Launch and connect

The chapter joins the ATF community and takes part in ATF programmes where they run.

What ATF provides

  • Direction and programme materials
  • Introductions to experts from the ATF network
  • A route into ATF programmes, currently the AI Challenge
  • ATF brand assets for chapter events
