Learn AI. Build for a real problem.

The ATF AI Challenge trains university students in applied AI, then sets them real problems from hospitals and companies to build against. It started in Ghana in 2023. With support from Google.org, it is scaling to Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, and South Africa.

How It Works

Training first. Then a real brief.

The Challenge is built around one idea: students learn fastest when the problem belongs to someone who needs it solved.

01

Train

Participants learn applied AI: working with data, using models, and turning them into a tool someone can use.

02

Take a real problem

Partner organisations set problem statements drawn from their own work — a hospital ward, a farm advisory service.

03

Build in teams

Teams build a minimum viable product against the problem they chose, with input from the partner who set it.

04

Show the work

Teams present working MVPs. In 2024, 450 students from 23 universities produced 22 of them.
